# Mailstream Flutter UI

A Flutter macOS front end for Mailstream, styled after macOS 26's Liquid Glass with Dia's islands. The Mailstream engine
(accounts, OAuth, sync, push, the cache, threading and AI tagging) runs inside this app's
process: `packages/mailstream` is the Dart binding of the `FFI/` crate (generated by
uniffi-dart), and its build hook bundles `libmailstream.dylib` and `libMailstreamKit.dylib`.
The UI lists accounts, mailboxes and conversations, reads previews, sender avatars and
messages (with their HTML), searches, marks, archives, trashes, tags, replies and sends new mail through it,
does Google Chat (the spaces of every chat account, their messages, sending and starting new
chats), manages accounts and settings, and follows the engine's change events instead of polling.
Mailstream.app need not run.

```sh
# at the repository root: release dylibs and the Dart bindings (packages/mailstream/lib/src, not committed)
CONFIG=release DART_OUT=$PWD/Flutter/packages/mailstream/lib/src ./scripts/ffi.sh
cd Flutter
flutter build macos --release           # build/macos/Build/Products/Release/Mailstream Flutter.app
../scripts/sign.sh "build/macos/Build/Products/Release/Mailstream Flutter.app"
```

Signing with the app's identifier and team lets the engine read the sign-ins Mailstream keeps
in the Keychain. The build is arm64 only. The app is not sandboxed.

The look is macOS 26's Liquid Glass laid out as islands, after the Dia browser: the whole window
is the system's glass (`NSGlassEffectView`, which `macos/Runner/MainFlutterWindow.swift` places
under the transparent Flutter view, through `macos_window_utils`), the sidebar sits on it, and the
list and the conversation are rounded white islands floating on it with a gap between them. The
toolbars' controls float on capsules of real system glass (`mailstream/glass` platform views
under them), the controls over a row on a raised capsule. Buttons, icon buttons, the search and
text fields and spinners are `macos_ui`'s; Material's remaining widgets are themed to match
(system font sizes, accent blue, gray fills, SF Symbols-style icons from `CupertinoIcons`). It
needs macOS 26.

The sidebar shows from 1200 dp wide (a rail below, whose menu button opens it). The list's
island fills the window; from 1000 dp opening a conversation splits it, the conversation's
island beside the list's, and closing it gives the list the width back. Narrower windows slide
the conversation's island over the list's. Either way a two-finger swipe to the right on the
trackpad drags the conversation out and closes it, springing from the fingers' velocity; the
close button and Esc slide it out on the same spring.

HTML mail is drawn by WebKit: `macos/Runner/MailHtmlView.swift` is a platform view
(`mailstream/html`) that runs none of the mail's scripts, opens links in the browser, loads
remote images without cookies, zooms out a body wider than the column, and reports its
content height so the body scrolls with the conversation.

| Path | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `packages/mailstream` | The engine's Dart binding and the build hook that bundles its libraries |
| `lib/backend/models.dart` | Accounts, mailboxes, conversations and messages, built from the engine's records |
| `macos/Runner/MailHtmlView.swift` | The WebKit platform view for HTML bodies |
| `lib/state/mail_state.dart` | `MailState`: selection, paged lists served from memory for 90 s, previews, search, the reader, and optimistic actions that roll back on failure |
| `lib/state/chat_state.dart` | `ChatState`: chat accounts and spaces, the open space's messages, sending and starting chats; re-read on the engine's `chat`/`chatMessages` events |
| `lib/ui/chat.dart` | The chat list, the open space (newest at the bottom, older pages loading at the top, messages in Chat's markup, videos playing and pictures showing in the log, other attachments as tiles, a composer: Return sends, ⇧Return a new line) and the New chat dialog (completing to the account's chat partners, then mail contacts) |
| `lib/ui/chat_markup.dart` | Google Chat's message markup (bold, italic, strike, code, fences, lists, quotes, links, mentions, escapes) as selectable text |
| `lib/ui/design.dart` | `MailTheme` (the `macos_ui` theme and Material's, in macOS colors and type sizes), motion, list and island metrics, stable account/topic/avatar colors |
| `lib/ui/glass.dart` | Islands, glass capsules (system glass under the controls), raised capsules, toolbar buttons, spinner |
| `lib/ui/shell.dart` | Islands layout, toolbars, search field, keys, snackbars |
| `lib/ui/sidebar.dart` | Mail-style sidebar and rail |
| `lib/ui/conversation_list.dart` | Fixed-height lazy list that loads the next page near its end, Smart Inbox bundles and its "Waiting on you" group, hover actions |
| `lib/ui/reader.dart` | Reading column, message cards and chat log, HTML bodies (quoted history cut out and folded), inline reply |
| `lib/ui/compose.dart` | The compose window for a new plain-text message (`mail.send`): From, To, Cc, Bcc (completing to contacts), Subject, body, attachments (the Attach button's open panel, files dropped on the window or pasted with ⌘V: `mailstream/files` in `macos/Runner/MainFlutterWindow.swift`) |
| `lib/ui/people.dart` | `PeopleCompletion`: people suggested under a recipient field as it is typed in (`contacts.search`, through `MailState.searchContacts`); ↑/↓, Return or Tab, Esc |
| `lib/ui/settings.dart` | Settings (⌘,): accounts (browser sign-in, rename, remove) and every key of the settings.json Mailstream.app and `mailstream config` share (`config.*` over FFI), following edits made elsewhere |
| `lib/ui/slide_layer.dart` | Bundles (and, in one pane, the reader) slide over the list, which stays mounted; trackpad swipe back |
| `lib/bench.dart` | The frame measurement `scripts/bench.sh flutter` runs (`MAILSTREAM_BENCH=scroll`): the list scrolls itself and every frame's `FrameTiming` is logged |

Keys: ↑/↓ or J/K move (beside an open conversation they open the next one), Return or Space
opens, Esc or ⌘[ goes back, E archives, ⌫ trashes, S stars, U toggles read, D resolves "Waiting on you" (or puts it back), R replies (⇧R or
A replies to all), ⌘N writes a new message, ⌘F searches, ⌘R refreshes, ⌘, opens Settings. In the compose window
⌘Return sends, ⌘V with files on the clipboard (copied in Finder) attaches them, and Esc or ⌘W
closes (asking first when something is written).

Not here: forwarding and attachments on replies; in Chat, threads,
reactions, editing and attachments on send.
Reply recipients shown in the composer are a preview; the app plans the real ones when it
sends.
